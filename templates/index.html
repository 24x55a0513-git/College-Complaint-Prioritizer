How It Works

A simple system for collecting, analyzing and prioritizing student complaints.

📝

1. Submit

Students submit their complaint with details such as category, urgency and number of students affected.

🧠

2. Analyze

The system calculates a priority score using urgency, category and affected students.

🚦

3. Prioritize

Complaints are automatically classified as Critical, High, Medium or Low priority.

Priority Levels

🔴 Critical 80 – 100
🟠 High 60 – 79
🟡 Medium 40 – 59
🟢 Low 0 – 39
